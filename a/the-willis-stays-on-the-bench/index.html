The case for the bench

Willis has averaged 16.10 points in the six games he has played. Goff has averaged 22.17 and Stroud 19.17, so on the season the two quarterbacks KnightRider_85 keeps starting have been the right two.

Willis has outscored a started quarterback three times: in week 1, when Sam Darnold left early and scored 0.52, by 0.44 over Stroud in week 5, and this week. Those three weeks add up to 28.62 points left on the bench. KnightRider_85 won and beat the median in weeks 1 and 5 without him, and this week the gap was too big for it to matter. Benching Willis has cost him points and no results.

The part that did cost something

Breece Hall, again

The more expensive decision was at running back. Breece Hall, who has not played since week 3, started for the second week in a row and scored 0.00 for the second week in a row. Last week the lineup also had Saquon Barkley in it without Barkley playing, and KnightRider_85 still won by the biggest margin of the week. This week Barkley played and scored 15.60, and the Hall slot was empty again.

Add the tight end and defence on the bench, AJ Barner (9.60 against Isaiah Likely's 2.70) and the Seahawks (13.00 against the Broncos' 7.00), and his best possible lineup was 182.30. That is 30.46 points more than he scored, the largest gap in the league this week, and 16.56 short of TheSoop. His bench scored 80.80, also the most in the league.

There was plenty to like in what he did start. Jaxon Smith-Njigba scored 30.30, Ollie Gordon 19.80 for the Dolphins in the same game as Willis, and Cairo Santos, who sat on the bench with the kicker slot empty in week 6, was in the lineup and kicked for 15.00. He is 9-5, level with TheSoop and geauxjack, one result behind ceefry.

The loss makes TheSoop 6-1 against KnightRider_85 all time. This week Sleeper projects Willis for 15.41, behind Darnold (17.02) and Goff (16.45) and ahead of Stroud, whose Texans are on bye.
