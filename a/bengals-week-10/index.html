Bengals 38, Steelers 18, Paycor Stadium

Five in a row

The Bengals beat Pittsburgh 38-18 on Sunday Night Football, their fifth straight win, and they are 7-2. Joe Burrow completed 35 of 44 passes for 409 yards and four touchdowns, 302 yards and three of the touchdowns in the first half. Ja'Marr Chase caught 11 passes for 231 yards, the fourth 200-yard game of his career, with catches of 53 and 43 yards. He did not score. Chase Brown caught two touchdowns, from two and four yards, and Drew Sample and Dohnte Meyers caught the others.

The Steelers did not score until Cincinnati led 17-0. The Bengals have now scored 30 or more in five straight games, which the team says is the longest such run in its history. Evan McPherson missed from 43 yards, which ended a run of 25 straight made field goals. Joe Flacco finished the game at quarterback.

According to the Bengals, Burrow is the second quarterback in NFL history with four or more total touchdowns in five consecutive games. The other is Peyton Manning, in 2004. We mention Manning only for the record, since the comparison runs the other way.
