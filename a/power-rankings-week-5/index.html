Marking our own homework

The top two swept and the third went 0-2

We moved ceefry from seventh to first last week, and he beat Willis’s Willies by 12.66 and cleared the median. TheSoop, our second, scored the most points of the week. Both went 2-0.

We had geauxjack third while he sat alone at the top of the table, and said a 5-1 start built on every starter landing would be hard to repeat with a thin bench and Justin Jefferson hurt. He scored 122.48, the lowest total of the week, and went 0-2. The miss was derrek777. We ranked him eighth, wrote that he was better than his record, and then he swept the defending champion. We should have ranked him like we meant it.

Where we disagree with the table

We disagree in three places. geauxjack is tied for second in the standings and seventh here, because his two best receivers are hurt, his bench projects last, and a 5-3 record is not much of a cushion behind that. KnightRider_85 is 4-4 and third in points and we have him last, because the roster that scored those points is no longer available to him. derrek777 is 4-4 and we have him fourth, because with Daniels back his roster is as strong as anyone's.

We also have the table's leader second. TheSoop and ceefry are separated by 4.3 points scored and 1.7 points of per-game strength, and the record gap is two results. Over ten more weeks we would take ceefry, narrowly, once he has a quarterback again.
