The Median Did
Its Job This Time

Last week the second result agreed with the first one ten times out of ten. This week it disagreed four times, and every one of them mattered.

Week 2

Four teams went 1-1

The piece that led this site last week was about the median matchup doing nothing at all. Every head-to-head result in week 1 was matched by its median result, five teams went 2-0, five went 0-2, and the standings became a list of teams sorted by points scored.

Week 2 was the version the rule was written for. Rancor Fajitas and OneManEra both lost their games and beat the median. Irrational Exuberance! and Fryder-Man: Brand New Day both won their games and missed it. Four teams finished 1-1 and the table stopped being a scoreboard.

The unlucky

Two teams scored well and drew the wrong opponent

ProfessorMerlyn put up 143.86, the fourth-highest total of the week, and lost by 25.59 because he was scheduled against the team that scored the most. OneManEra put up 141.98, lost by 3.46, and cleared the median by 0.58.

Without the second result both of those teams go to 0-2 on weeks that were not bad. With it they are 1-1, and the difference over a fourteen-week season is the difference between chasing the playoff field and being in it. Merlyn has now had the two unluckiest weeks in the league back to back, and is 1-3.

The lucky

Two teams won and got nothing extra for it

geauxjack beat the highest-scoring team in the league and still finished the week 1-1, because 140.82 was below the midpoint. ceefry beat the defending champion by 3.31 and did the same thing on 139.73. Both of those are wins that a league without a median would have counted in full.

That is the trade the rule makes. It gives a result back to the team that scored well and takes one away from the team that won a low-scoring game, and across a season it moves the standings closer to the points column. In week 1 it had nothing to correct. In week 2 it corrected four teams.

The whole week fitted inside 38.25 points from top to bottom, which is barely more than half the 67.78 of week 1. When everybody scores the same amount the midpoint decides a lot.
