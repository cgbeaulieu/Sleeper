OneManEra

The bench quarterback starts

For five weeks Kirk Cousins sat on OneManEra's bench and outscored one of the two quarterbacks in front of him, and for two weeks we wrote it up. In week 6 he was in the lineup at quarterback and scored 19.52. Drake Maye started at superflex and scored 24.30, and OneManEra beat evspade 141.42 to 136.11, the closest game of the week, and beat the median as well.

The reason Cousins started is not a happy one. Jalen Hurts did not play in week 6, and on Saturday the Eagles put him on injured reserve after a blood clot was found in his calf. On Monday the team and his agent said he has deep vein thrombosis and blood clots in both lungs, that he is clinically stable, and that he is expected to make a full recovery. The Associated Press, citing an unnamed source, reported that he is expected to miss the rest of the season. The team has not said that.

Six weeks

How the three quarterbacks have scored

Through six weeks Cousins has 116.08 points, an average of 19.35. Maye has 124.52, most of it in the last three weeks, when he scored 29.16, 39.46 and 24.30. Hurts scored 86.52 in five games. Cousins outscored Maye in each of the first three weeks and Hurts in weeks 4 and 5, which is how the streak reached five. It ends here because he was in the lineup.

The two-week run that turned the season around also had help from the waiver wire. On Sunday morning OneManEra picked up Dameon Pierce for the Cardinals defence, and Pierce scored 15.00 at running back. Spencer Shrader, picked up to replace Jake Bates for the Detroit bye, kicked for 9.00. OneManEra was 2-6 after week 4. He has won four straight results since and is level with brunosilvadev at 6-6, fifth and sixth in the table, in the last two playoff places.

Week 7

The league leader

The streak runs into ceefry this week, and it is the widest projected game on the slate. Sleeper projects ceefry for 185.9 and OneManEra for 163.0, with Maye at quarterback (23.59) and Cousins at superflex (15.92). The two are 2-2 all time. OneManEra's healthy bench is the thinnest in the league, with Hurts, Alec Pierce, Michael Pittman and Jacory Croskey-Merritt all on IR, so the eleven who start are close to the whole team.
