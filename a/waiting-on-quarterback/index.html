Worked Last Year

The other side

The panic has a counterexample

The read on this draft is that the league underpriced quarterbacks. Thirty came off the board for twenty starting spots, only one went in the first round, and twelve are sitting on benches. That is all true and it is in the numbers.

It is also exactly what happened in 2025, and the teams that waited won.

The podium was swept by the three managers who waited longest, and the three teams that spent premium capital at the position filled out the bottom of the bracket in order.

The caveat

Waiting did not get anyone in

Two teams also waited and missed the playoffs. geauxjack took his first quarterback at 36 and finished eighth. SAMonFLY waited until 52 and finished last at 8-20.

Waiting was not what got teams into the bracket. Among the teams that got in, it tracked with how far they went. Those are different claims and the second one is the weaker of the two.

One season and ten teams is not enough to make a rule out of. It is enough to say that the loudest read on this draft has been tested once in this league and came out backwards.

2026

The people repeating the strategy

evspade took his first quarterback at pick 50 this year after taking one at 54 last year, so the champion is running the same plan that won him the title. KnightRider_85 waited until 66 after waiting until 82 last year, and last year that produced his best finish in five seasons.

The two managers being questioned hardest for their quarterback rooms are the two who finished first and third the last time this format was played. That does not make them right. It does mean the draft-day consensus and the only available evidence disagree, and Week 1 starts settling it.
