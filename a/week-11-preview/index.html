Where it stands

Four weeks and eight results remain before the playoffs, and six of ten teams get in. geauxjack and TheSoop are 14-6, in that order on points. ceefry is 12-8, KnightRider_85 11-9 and evspade 10-10. derrek777 holds sixth at 9-11. ProfessorMerlyn, CaptainClark and OneManEra are a result behind him at 8-12, and brunosilvadev is 6-14. The trade deadline is this week.

Atlanta, Cleveland, Green Bay, the Rams, New England and Seattle are on bye. OneManEra loses eight players, including Drake Maye, who is already listed out with an ankle injury, Drake London, Tucker Kraft, Zach Charbonnet and both of his defences. ceefry loses Bijan Robinson, Michael Penix, Kyren Williams, Jadarian Price, DeMario Douglas and Hunter Henry. ProfessorMerlyn loses six as well, Blake Corum and the Rams defence among them. KnightRider_85 loses Jaxon Smith-Njigba, his kicker and his defence. geauxjack loses nobody he could have started.

TheSoop v geauxjack is the game for first. Both are 14-6, and they are second and first in projected score this week, 177.4 and 183.2. geauxjack leads the series 5-2 and scored 210.90 last week, the second-highest score of the season. Sleeper projects his two quarterbacks, Dak Prescott and Trevor Lawrence, for 44.71 between them, and TheSoop's, Josh Allen and Joe Burrow, for 53.33. TheSoop is without Harold Fannin and KC Concepcion, who are on bye with Cleveland, and geauxjack gets D'Andre Swift back from Chicago's bye. The Bengals play on Monday night, with Burrow and the Bengals defence on one side of this game and Ja'Marr Chase on the other.

KnightRider_85 v CaptainClark is the other game projected 5.8 apart, and it is the other way round from the series. KnightRider_85 has won seven of their eight meetings, but CaptainClark has won four straight results and has Caleb Williams and Chris Godwin back from their bye, while Seattle's bye takes Smith-Njigba, Jason Myers and the Seahawks defence from KnightRider_85.

ProfessorMerlyn v brunosilvadev is the Stafford custody game, and Matthew Stafford is on bye. brunosilvadev took him at pick 48 in the draft and has beaten ProfessorMerlyn in all three of their meetings. ProfessorMerlyn projects 27.0 ahead, with Bucky Irving and Jonah Coleman back from their bye and Patrick Mahomes projected for 23.51. brunosilvadev's best lineup has J.J. McCarthy and Fernando Mendoza at the two quarterback slots.

ceefry v evspade sets the team with the most players on bye after OneManEra against the only team with nobody listed out. evspade leads the series 4-2 and gets Bo Nix back. ceefry's projected 128.9 does not count Baker Mayfield, who is questionable with a foot and ankle injury. If Mayfield plays he replaces Penix, who is on bye, in the superflex slot, and the gap closes.

OneManEra v derrek777 has the widest projected margin, 48.1. derrek777 won their only meeting. With Jordan Love and Deshaun Watson on bye, Jayden Daniels is his only quarterback with a game and Sleeper's best lineup puts Rachaad White at superflex. OneManEra is in the same position with Kirk Cousins, and his best lineup puts Courtland Sutton there. A win for derrek777 keeps him in sixth. A loss could drop him out of it.

Waivers

Who is still out there

Three teams are a quarterback short this week: derrek777, OneManEra and ceefry, if Mayfield does not play. Joe Milton of Philadelphia is the free agent quarterback with the highest projection, 12.75, after scoring 14.16 in his only game this season. That is more than Sleeper projects for White or Sutton at superflex. Kyle McCord of Miami is projected for 11.10 after scoring 12.76 in his only game.

KnightRider_85 also needs a kicker, because Jason Myers, added last week, is on bye with Seattle.
