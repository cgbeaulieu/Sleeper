The same receiver, the other way round

In week 2, CaptainClark put Puka Nacua in his flex after the Rams had declared him inactive. Nacua scored 0.00 and the Buttercups left 99.00 points on the bench. Nacua then missed week 3 as well.

The Rams cleared him to play against Philadelphia on Friday, with no injury designation, and Sean McVay said they would have to manage his snap count. CaptainClark left him on the bench. Nacua scored 25.70.

In one of the two receiver spots he started Terry McLaurin, who tweaked a hamstring in Friday's practice. Washington played Indianapolis in London on Sunday morning and declared McLaurin inactive before kickoff. He scored 0.00.

In his defence

The London problem

The McLaurin decision was harder than it looks on a box score. There was optimism over the weekend that he would play through the hamstring, and the inactive list for a London game arrives early on a Sunday morning in the United States. Sleeper locks each player at his own kickoff, so there was a window to swap him, and it was a short one. Benching Nacua on a snap count after two missed games was a reasonable call that did not work.

CeeDee Lamb scored 37.80, the second-best score by any starter in the league this week, and the roster averages the fourth-best lineup in the league per game. CaptainClark also reached last year's final on three waiver adds all season. The team is good. In two of four weeks the lineup has had a receiver in it who did not play, and both times the points were on his own bench.
