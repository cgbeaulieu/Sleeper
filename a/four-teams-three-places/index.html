Nobody is eliminated. brunosilvadev can still reach 12-16, and that is enough if the teams above him lose often enough. The top three are close to certain, and TheSoop has a 95% chance of one of the two first-round byes, with geauxjack at 85%. The race worth watching is the four teams in the middle. Between them they are likely to fill three places, and the model is least sure about the team that won the title last year.

The bubble

Why it is evspade

evspade is 11-11, level with derrek777 and a result ahead of ProfessorMerlyn, and he is still the least likely of the four to get in. There are two reasons. He has scored the fewest points of the four, 1589.6, so he loses any tie in the table. And by his opponents' records his schedule is the hardest left in the league: geauxjack this week, derrek777 in week 13 and TheSoop in week 14. TheSoop has never beaten him in six attempts, which is one thing in his favour.

He won his game last week 114.56 to 102.82 and missed the median. Justin Herbert, whom he claimed for $3 before the week, scored 23.44 on his bench while Daniel Jones started at superflex and scored 5.06. With Herbert in, he scores 132.94, the median recomputes to 134.20, and he still misses it by 1.26. Nobody has ever won this league twice, and the model gives him about a coin flip to get the chance.

ceefry is fourth at 12-10 and has lost four straight results, both of week 10's against TheSoop and the median and both of last week's. He lost to evspade by 11.74 while Baker Mayfield, questionable all week, scored 19.58 on his bench and Joe Milton started at superflex for 6.34. Mayfield for Milton would have won that game by 1.50, and ceefry would be 13-9. He won the title from the sixth seed in 2024. Lamar Jackson and Brock Bowers are still listed out, and he plays ProfessorMerlyn, KnightRider_85 and CaptainClark.

derrek777 beat OneManEra by 64.46 last week, the widest margin of the week, and went 2-0. He has more points than ceefry and ProfessorMerlyn. His schedule runs TheSoop, evspade and ProfessorMerlyn, so he plays two of the other three bubble teams himself. No manager who joined after 2022 has won the title in his first season.

ProfessorMerlyn is seventh at 10-12 and the model still likes him more than evspade, because his team has been better than his record. He scored 194.12 last week, the high score, and his healthy per-game strength, 170.2, is second in the league behind TheSoop. He plays ceefry, geauxjack and derrek777, which means he controls two of the three games between bubble teams.
