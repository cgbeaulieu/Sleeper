Bengals 34, Falcons 17, Madrid

Four in a row

The Bengals beat Atlanta 34-17 at the Estadio Santiago Bernabéu on Sunday, their fourth straight win, and they are 6-2. Joe Burrow completed 24 of 29 passes for 271 yards and three touchdowns with no interceptions, and scored a fourth himself on a one-yard sneak. Ja'Marr Chase caught 10 passes for 155 yards, eight of them for 142 in the first half, and scored from 47 yards. Tee Higgins caught five for 32 and scored twice, from four and eight yards.

The defence held Michael Penix to 162 passing yards and sealed it late when linebacker Barrett Carter intercepted him at the Atlanta 43. Bijan Robinson ran for 100 yards and a touchdown, and Atlanta was penalised 12 times. The Bengals play the Steelers at home on Sunday night.

A week after he threw three interceptions and won anyway, Burrow threw none. We were not worried, and we would like that on the record now that it no longer needs saying.
