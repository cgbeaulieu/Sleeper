Marking our own homework

Two calls went the wrong way

Our top five went 6-4 in week 10 and our bottom five went 4-6. TheSoop, geauxjack and KnightRider_85 won both results. ceefry, our second, and ProfessorMerlyn, our fourth, lost both.

The two disagreements we were proudest of both went against us. We had ProfessorMerlyn fourth while he was eighth in the table, and he went 0-2. We put CaptainClark last because of who he had available in week 10, Sleeper projected him for 119.2, and he scored 183.08, beat ProfessorMerlyn by 39.06 and went 2-0. We wrote last week that his ranking could move back as quickly as it fell, and it has. The third, evspade seventh while fifth in the table, came out even: he went 1-1 and is now 10-10.
