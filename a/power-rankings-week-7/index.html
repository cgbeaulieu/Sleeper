Marking our own homework

Better than last week, which was not hard

Our top five went 6-4 in week 6 and our bottom five went 4-6, which is the right way round and about as much as we can claim. ceefry, our first, went 2-0. OneManEra, our tenth, also went 2-0, for the second week running.

The three places where we disagreed most with the table did not go well. We had CaptainClark sixth and derrek777 fourth on the strength of their rosters, and both went 0-2. We had brunosilvadev seventh because his bench was empty, and he lost by 6.92 while his bench scored 38.56. We are keeping two of those three calls, for the reasons below.

What moved

Where we disagree with the table

The table has geauxjack and TheSoop level at 7-5 behind KnightRider_85, and we agree with the order at the top. The disagreements are in the middle. evspade is 5-7, seventh in the table, and we have him fifth because his healthy roster projects fourth and Sleeper likes his lineup for this week more than anyone's but ceefry's. derrek777 is 4-8 and we have him sixth for the same reason we had him fourth last week. OneManEra is 6-6 and we have him eighth, because four straight results have not changed the fact that his roster scores the fewest points per week in the league.

Week 6 was the lowest-scoring week of the season. The median was 135.34 and the week's high score was 153.56, both the lowest of 2026, which helps explain how a lineup with two empty running back spots won by the biggest margin.
