The record

Four seasons, four champions

This league has crowned a champion four times and it has been a different manager every time. Nobody has gone back to back, and only one of the four has even reached the final the following season.

The middle two titles went to teams that had no business winning them on the regular season. Milldog86 scored the seventh-most points in a ten-team league and his team won anyway. ceefry got in as the last seed at 6-8 and then dropped 246.3 points in the wild card round, still the highest single week in league history, on his way to the title.

The defense

What happens the year after

Three champions have had a chance to defend. One left the league, one made the final again, and one missed the playoffs entirely.

2022 → 23

OneManEra came closest

He followed the title with a 7-7 season and lost in the final. That is the only time a defending champion has reached the championship game.

2023 → 24

Milldog86 never got the chance

Milldog86 died unexpectedly during the 2023 postseason. Chris managed his roster through the rest of the run, the team won the championship, and the winnings went to his fiancée. It is the only title in this league that its manager did not live to see.

2024 → 25

ceefry missed the playoffs

The defending champion went 10-18 and finished eighth of ten. He is back this year with Lamar Jackson at pick 19 and a wide receiver in the superflex.

The favorite

evspade is a different kind of champion

The first three titles were upsets to some degree. The 2025 season was not. evspade took the 1 seed, scored the most points in the league, went 24-4, and then won the thing. It is the only time the best regular-season team has converted.

Across four seasons evspade has won 69% of his games, the best career mark in the league by a wide margin. The next closest full-time manager is at 57%.

He has also never traded, which puts him in a league of ten. He spent $61 of his $100 FAAB in 2025 and made 21 adds, sitting mid-pack in both. The 2025 title did not come from working the wire harder than everyone else.

Week 1 opens him against Skinned Knee Varsity at 56%, the second-largest edge on the board behind a first-year manager. If the defense is going to end four seasons of turnover, it starts as the favorite.
