The founders

Five seasons, no ring

Six managers have been here since the beginning. OneManEra won the first title in 2022, back when the league had eight teams. The other five have played every season this league has existed and have nothing to show for it.

Both of the other champions in league history joined later. ceefry and evspade both arrived in 2023 and both have rings. Milldog86 arrived in 2022 and holds the 2023 title. He died during that postseason, and his team finished the run and won it.

The best of them

geauxjack beats everyone and finishes nowhere

geauxjack has the strongest head-to-head profile in the league. He has never lost to KnightRider_85 in five seasons, and he holds a 7-1 record against CaptainClark. His .536 career winning percentage is the best among the ringless and third best overall.

He has also missed the playoffs in two of the last three seasons, including 2025 when he went 10-18. Owning individual opponents has not translated into anything that shows up in the record books.

The closest call

CaptainClark got there doing nothing

CaptainClark has the lowest career winning percentage in the league at .321 and has missed the playoffs in two of five seasons. He also reached the 2025 championship game on three roster adds and zero FAAB spent all year.

He made three moves in fourteen weeks and got within one game of a title. TheSoop made 43 adds that same season and missed the playoffs.

Both of them are back for 2026. Bison Circle Buttercups opens as a slight favorite against Rancor Fajitas, and Who Dey and the Blowfish opens at 49% against Fryder-Man.
