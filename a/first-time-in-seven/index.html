Week 5

Six straight, then one

Before Sunday, KnightRider_85 had played geauxjack six times in the regular season and lost all six. In week 5 the two of them posted the two highest scores in the league and drew each other, and Willis’s Willies won 193.38 to 187.08. He is now 1-6 against geauxjack all time.

It is also the week after we ranked the Willies last. Saquon Barkley, Breece Hall and DeVonta Smith were all out, and they still are. Jared Goff scored 28.94 at superflex, Isaiah Likely 28.90 at tight end and Malik Nabers 26.60. C.J. Stroud added 21.94, Jaxon Smith-Njigba 21.80, and Ollie Gordon, the running back we used as evidence that this roster was out of options, scored 17.30. The Broncos defence, a free-agent pickup during the week, scored 23.00.

He did not get every decision right. Malik Willis scored 22.38 on the bench, which is 0.44 more than Stroud scored as the starter, so with four quarterbacks to choose from he started the wrong two by less than half a point. He benched the Seahawks defence (13.00) for the Broncos one, and that was worth ten points. He won by 6.30.

The other side

geauxjack had the points on his bench

geauxjack scored 187.08, which would have beaten every other team in the league this week. Parker Washington scored 27.90 in the flex, the Packers defence 26.00 and Trevor Lawrence 23.24. Ja'Marr Chase, back from his concussion, scored 17.70 and Justin Jefferson, back from his ankle, 16.80.

His bench scored 71.88, the most in the league. Jacoby Brissett scored 27.28 there while Dak Prescott started at superflex and scored 14.64. Mike Evans scored 25.30 on the bench while George Kittle started in the flex and scored 5.40. Either swap on its own wins the game: Brissett for Prescott makes it 199.72, Evans for Kittle makes it 206.98. Neither moves the median, so geauxjack would have gone 2-0 and KnightRider_85 1-1. Picking Brissett over Prescott takes hindsight, and the result still went the other way.

He finished 1-1, the unlucky split of the week, and is 6-4 and tied for second.
