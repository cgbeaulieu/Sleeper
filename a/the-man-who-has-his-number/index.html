Week 4

The leader scored the fewest

geauxjack went into week 4 at 5-1, alone at the top of the table, against a ProfessorMerlyn team that was 1-5. He scored 122.48, the lowest total in the league, and lost by 6.08. The median was 157.92, so it was 0-2, and he drops into a three-way tie at 5-3.

Merlyn won with 128.56, the second-lowest score of the week, which is why he finished 1-1 and not 2-0. It was still a win over a team with a winning record against most of this league across five seasons. geauxjack is 6-0 all time against KnightRider_85 and 7-1 against CaptainClark. Against Merlyn he is now 3-5. The only other current manager ahead of him is evspade, at 4-2 including this season.

How it went wrong

Chase left early and the points were on the bench

Ja'Marr Chase caught three passes for 27 yards against Jacksonville, had his facemask pulled in the first half, and was ruled out with a concussion. He finished on 4.70. Justin Jefferson was already out. Parker Washington scored 1.50 in one flex spot, and Brian Robinson scored 27.20 on the bench.

Robinson in place of Washington makes it 148.18, which beats Merlyn. It does not reach the median, which stays at 157.92 when recomputed, so the best version of geauxjack's week was 1-1. That would have left him tied with TheSoop at 6-2 and Merlyn at 0-2.

Kyle Monangai's 30.50 was the best thing on the roster, and Dak Prescott added 18.10 at superflex. The CPU took Chase for geauxjack at 1.3 in the draft, and before Sunday he had been one of the reasons the record was 5-1.
