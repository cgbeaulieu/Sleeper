The quarterback count

Two teams with one passer each

ceefry has three quarterbacks on his roster. Baker Mayfield dislocated his thumb in week 3 and is out for at least three weeks. Lamar Jackson sprained an ankle on Sunday, and the latest reporting gives him only an outside chance of playing against Atlanta. That leaves Tyler Shough. Sleeper's projections have ceefry starting a tight end, Juwan Johnson, at superflex.

ProfessorMerlyn has the same problem from the schedule. Bryce Young plays for Carolina and Patrick Mahomes for Kansas City, so both are on bye, and Justin Herbert is the only quarterback he has left. His projected superflex is Blake Corum.

Both can be fixed on waivers. Sleeper projects Jalon Daniels, who is starting for Tampa Bay in Mayfield's place, at 16.2 this week, Michael Penix at 15.3, and Tyson Bagent, starting for Chicago with Caleb Williams out, at 14.8. All three are free agents. If Jackson sits, his backup Tyler Huntley is also unowned.

The games

Two long losing streaks on the line

KnightRider_85 against geauxjack. KnightRider_85 is 0-6 against him all time. Ja'Marr Chase is in concussion protocol and Justin Jefferson is out, and Sleeper projects the Willies ahead, 167.0 to 156.8, even with Saquon Barkley, Breece Hall and DeVonta Smith all unavailable. The projection puts Ollie Gordon at running back.

TheSoop against evspade. TheSoop is 0-5 against the defending champion. He is projected ahead, 171.8 to 165.3. Joe Burrow plays this week and then has the Bengals' week 6 bye, which TheSoop will cover with Geno Smith and some reluctance.

CaptainClark against ceefry. The closest projection of the week, 165.7 to 163.8 for CaptainClark, and the reason it is close is ceefry's quarterback situation. Clark is 3-2 against him all time. Puka Nacua, back from a groin injury, projects as the Buttercups' best receiver.

ProfessorMerlyn against derrek777. The first meeting, and the widest projection, 161.6 to 143.5 for derrek777. That number assumes Jayden Daniels comes back from his dislocated elbow as Washington expects. Merlyn's 143.5 assumes a running back at superflex.

brunosilvadev against OneManEra. brunosilvadev is 1-2 against him. OneManEra is projected ahead, 160.0 to 154.7. If Jayden Daniels returns, Marcus Mariota goes back to the Washington bench, which leaves brunosilvadev with Matthew Stafford and Daniel Jones. OneManEra has to decide whether Kirk Cousins, who has outscored a starting quarterback from the bench three weeks running, gets a start.
