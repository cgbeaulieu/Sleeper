Marking our own homework

Last week's number one went 0-2

We put LeBron Offensive Foul first seven days ago on the strength of the best roster and the deepest bench in the league. evspade then got 0.80 out of Jaxson Dart, lost by 3.31 and dropped to 2-2. We also put Willis’s Willies fifth. They are 4-0 and nobody else in the league is better than 3-1.

The thing we got right was the reason to like the Willies at all, which was that a roster with four quarterbacks on it has an answer when one of them gets hurt. Sam Darnold did not play. C.J. Stroud started in the superflex and scored 17.52, Jared Goff added 29.78, and the quarterback room went from 16.96 in week 1 to 47.30 in week 2.

What moved

The table and this list finally agree about something

Willis’s Willies lead both. That is the first time all season the top of the standings and the top of these rankings have been the same team, and it took an undefeated start to do it.

Underneath that it is still messy. Three of the four teams we ranked in the top four last week are 2-2 or worse, and the team we ranked eighth posted the highest score of the week. Two weeks is not much to go on, which is the argument for leaning on roster strength, and roster strength has now been wrong about ceefry twice.
