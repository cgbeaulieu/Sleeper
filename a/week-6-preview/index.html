The byes

Six Willies off at once

Cincinnati, Detroit, Miami and Minnesota all have the week off. Two teams in this league, ProfessorMerlyn and CaptainClark, have nobody from those four. KnightRider_85 has six: Malik Willis, Ollie Gordon, Malik Washington and his kicker Riley Patterson from Miami, Jared Goff from Detroit and Chase Brown from Cincinnati. With Saquon Barkley and Breece Hall also listed out, he has no running back available, and Sleeper's projection for his best lineup is 112.6, the lowest in the league by 27.2. He still has two healthy quarterbacks, C.J. Stroud and Sam Darnold, which after five weeks of jokes about his quarterback room is the part of the roster that works.

geauxjack loses Ja'Marr Chase, Justin Jefferson, Jordan Addison and Evan McPherson. evspade loses Kyler Murray and Sam LaPorta, which makes Jameis Winston his superflex. derrek777 loses Jahmyr Gibbs and the Bengals defence he claimed for $1 last week. TheSoop loses Joe Burrow and will start Geno Smith, and the house has no further comment.

The games

One coin flip and one mismatch

TheSoop against ProfessorMerlyn. The closest projection of the week, 173.0 to 172.8 for Merlyn. TheSoop is 3-4 against him all time. Merlyn gets Patrick Mahomes and Bryce Young back from the week 5 bye and has Justin Herbert and Tyler Huntley behind them, four quarterbacks for two slots. TheSoop has Josh Allen and Geno Smith, and David Montgomery is out with a groin injury.

KnightRider_85 against derrek777. Their first meeting, and the widest projection of the week: derrek777 ahead, 141.7 to 112.6. Jayden Daniels played his first game back last week and scored 33.26. If Barkley returns, the Willies have one running back. If he does not, the waiver wire is where the second one comes from, and Barkley's own backup, Dameon Pierce, is a free agent here.

CaptainClark against geauxjack. Sleeper has the Buttercups ahead, 167.1 to 161.9, mostly because geauxjack loses four players to byes. Puka Nacua projects as the best receiver in this game at 24.6. CeeDee Lamb is listed questionable with a quad injury. geauxjack has won seven of their eight meetings.

ceefry against brunosilvadev. The highest projection of the week, 194.6 to 167.1 for ceefry, and that is without Lamar Jackson, who may return. They are 2-2 all time. brunosilvadev loses Aaron Jones, Jameson Williams and the Vikings defence to byes, and his bench scored 15.08 last week, the least in the league.

OneManEra against evspade. They are 3-3 all time. Sleeper has evspade ahead, 158.0 to 139.8. OneManEra loses Amon-Ra St. Brown, Tee Higgins and his kicker, and this week his projected lineup has Kirk Cousins at superflex instead of on the bench.
