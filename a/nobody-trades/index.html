Nobody Trades

Four completed seasons have produced four trades league-wide, all of them in 2023. Any plan that depends on making a deal is betting against the entire history of this league.

The number

Four trades in four seasons

This league has completed four seasons. It has produced four trades. All four happened in 2023, and they involved TheSoop, OneManEra, KnightRider_85 and SAMonFLY. In 2022, 2024 and 2025 the league made zero trades of any kind.

Milldog86 won the 2023 title, the one season anybody traded, without making a trade himself.

The trade deadline this year is Week 11 and a deal needs five veto votes to be blocked after a two-day review. None of that machinery has been used since 2023.

What this means for 2026

Two teams have a problem the market will not fix

Skinned Knee Varsity comes out of the draft with Matthew Stafford and Daniel Jones as the only quarterbacks on the roster, in a format that starts two. One bye week or one injury puts brunosilvadev in a bad spot. He also finished 5th last season and worked the waiver wire as hard as anyone in the league, so he is not a manager who sits still.

The obvious line is that he will have to trade for a quarterback. That line predicts something that has happened four times in this league, all of them three years ago. The waiver wire is the realistic answer, and FAAB is where this gets decided.

The other half

Working the wire does not win either

If trading is off the table, aggressive roster churn looks like the remaining lever. The 2025 season says it is not much of one.
