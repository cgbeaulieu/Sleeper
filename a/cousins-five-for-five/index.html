OneManEra

Five weeks running

Last week we wrote that Kirk Cousins had outscored one of OneManEra's two starting quarterbacks in each of the first four weeks. In week 5 he scored 13.72 on the bench. Jalen Hurts started at superflex and scored 13.00, so the streak is five, by the smallest margin yet.

That is 96.56 points in five weeks from a player Sleeper projected for 39.5 over the whole season. The swap was worth 0.72 this week, and OneManEra won both results without it, because Drake Maye scored 39.46 and the Browns defence he picked up scored 21.00. Over the season, starting the better two of the three would have added 37.94 points. Only in week 2 would it have changed a result.

In week 6, Sleeper's projections take Cousins' side. They have Maye at 22.86, Cousins at 18.05 and Hurts at 17.49, so OneManEra's projected lineup has Cousins at superflex. Last week the same projections had him 2.6 points behind Hurts.

Elsewhere on the bench

He was not the only one

evspade claimed Jameis Winston for $2 in week 4 after derrek777 dropped him. Winston scored 20.00 on the bench that week and 26.74 on it this week, while Bo Nix started and scored 8.42. evspade won the game anyway, against TheSoop, by 13.13. Kyler Murray and the Vikings are on bye in week 6, so Winston will start.

ProfessorMerlyn added Tyler Huntley as a free agent on Thursday afternoon, before Baltimore announced that Lamar Jackson would miss the game. That was the best-timed pickup of the week. Huntley started for the Ravens and scored 25.04, on Merlyn's bench, with Blake Corum at superflex for 16.20. Merlyn won 2-0 regardless. If Jackson plays in week 6, Huntley goes back to being a backup.

geauxjack had the costliest one. Jacoby Brissett scored 27.28 on his bench and Dak Prescott 14.64 in the lineup, and that difference would have beaten KnightRider_85. It is in this week's recap.
