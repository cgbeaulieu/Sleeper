Bengals Week 4:
428 Yards And A Loss

Joe Burrow threw for 428 yards, Ja'Marr Chase left with a concussion after a facemask, and Cincinnati lost to Jacksonville by five. Plus the best free-agent add of the week, made on a Saturday night.

Jaguars 22, Bengals 17

A yard short at the 12

Joe Burrow completed 39 of 54 passes for 428 yards, threw a touchdown and two interceptions, and ran for another score. Tee Higgins caught 11 passes for 157 yards, season highs in both. Cincinnati still lost at Paycor Stadium and is 2-2.

Jacksonville went up 12 with 6:35 left. The Bengals scored with under four minutes to go to make it five, got the ball back, and drove to the Jaguars' 12. On fourth-and-10 with 50 seconds left, Burrow found Chase Brown for nine yards. Rayuan Lane III made the tackle a yard short of the marker.

Burrow, afterwards: "We're not here for moral victories." This site agrees with him and would add that 428 yards is not the part that needs fixing.

Ja'Marr Chase

The facemask, and what came after it

Chase had three catches for 27 yards when he was pulled down by the facemask in the first half and his head hit the ground. He was evaluated, ruled out with a concussion at halftime, and is in the protocol this week.

On their postgame show, Jake Liscow and Joe Goodberry called it a bad facemask followed by an illegal hit on a defenceless player. One of them said he had been worried about Chase's neck from the way his head was turned. Both said they wanted to see his teammates respond more forcefully to a play they considered dangerous, and neither thought they had.

Higgins then hurt an adductor in the fourth quarter. He is considered day-to-day, which matters a great deal with Chase out.

The postgame show

What else went wrong, by their count

Liscow and Goodberry thought the offence had volume without rhythm. Burrow's yardage came on a day the run game produced almost nothing, 15 carries for 23 yards by their count, and Chase Brown's own line was nine carries for 10 yards. They were unhappy with red-zone efficiency, with a defence that could not stop Jacksonville in the short and intermediate middle of the field or get off the field on third down, and with officiating they described as inconsistent from start to finish.

Their longer-running concerns are Zac Taylor's play-calling and Orlando Brown's pass protection. They described the loss as another "almost".

Who Dey and the Blowfish

The Saturday night add

On the night before week 4 kicked off, TheSoop picked up Emanuel Wilson, a Seattle running back, as a free agent for nothing. Wilson had scored 0.30, 12.20 and 1.40 in the first three weeks, and nobody else in the league had him. derrek777 had put in a $2 claim for him in week 2, which Sleeper rejected because his roster was full.

On Sunday Wilson carried 21 times for 81 yards and a touchdown and caught three passes for 39 yards and another touchdown. That is 27.00 points, the most by any player added in week 4. It was a very good read of a backfield and the house would like it on the record.

The 27.00 was scored on the bench. David Montgomery started in the flex and scored 4.30. TheSoop won both results anyway, and with Wilson in that slot the score would have been 197.04 and the results the same. Wilson is in the projected lineup this week at 14.9.
