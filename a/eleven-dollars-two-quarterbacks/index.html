4th and 35

A sensible plan and the wrong half of it

Jayden Daniels dislocated his elbow against Dallas in week 2. Washington has called him week-to-week and kept him off injured reserve, which leaves derrek777 with a hole in one of his two quarterback slots and no date for filling it. His answer on waivers was to buy two quarterbacks: $7 for Jameis Winston and $4 for Deshaun Watson. Two other claims, $2 on Emanuel Wilson and $0 on Rashod Bateman, failed.

That is a reasonable way to cover an injury in superflex, where a zero in the second quarterback slot costs more than anywhere else. He started Winston, the one he paid more for, and Winston scored 6.62. Watson scored 22.26 on the bench.

The quarterback swap on its own would not have changed the week. It takes him to 151.24, which still loses to TheSoop's 161.74, and because his higher score moves the median up to 152.82 he misses that too by 1.58. The other gap on the bench was the Jacksonville defence, which scored 21.00 while the Giants defence he started scored 8.00. With both changes he scores 164.24, wins his game, and the median recomputes to 156.36, which he beats. That result would have pushed brunosilvadev below the new median and turned two 2-0 weeks, TheSoop's and bruno's, into 1-1.

Picking defences in hindsight is easy, so the fairer reading is that the quarterback decision cost him 15.64 points and the defence decision cost him 13.00, and he needed both to go the other way. He did get 41.90 out of Jahmyr Gibbs, the best score by any starter in the league this week, and he is the only manager other than brunosilvadev to spend a dollar this season.

Skinned Knee Varsity

The $13 defence

brunosilvadev has spent $30 of his $100, more than the rest of the league combined. His week 2 claim was $13 for the Minnesota Vikings defence, and it scored 23.00 in week 3, the best defensive score on any roster in the league. He swept for the second week running and beat Willis’s Willies by 15.72.

The other bid on that defence came from TheSoop, at $0. TheSoop had dropped the Vikings in week 2 to pick up the Cincinnati defence, and in week 3 the Bengals defence scored 5.00 for him. This site stands by the decision.

The last time we checked, the paid moves had not returned much. That has changed. Across three weeks the league has spent $41 of a possible $1,000, and two managers have spent all of it.

History

What spending has bought before

In 2025, CaptainClark reached the final on three adds and no FAAB, and brunosilvadev spent his whole $100 and finished fifth. Spending has never been what separates teams in this league. This year the two spenders are 4-2 and 2-4.
