Marking our own homework

Nine and one

Our top five went 9-1 in week 11 and our bottom five went 1-9. TheSoop, KnightRider_85, ProfessorMerlyn and derrek777 won both results, and geauxjack, our second, split. evspade won the only result anyone in our bottom five won.

All three of our disagreements with the table went our way. We kept ProfessorMerlyn fourth while he was seventh in the table, and he scored the week's high and went 2-0. We had ceefry seventh while he was third, and he went 0-2. We had KnightRider_85 a place above the table, and he went 2-0. We are keeping all three, because three weeks from the playoffs the table still has ProfessorMerlyn and ceefry the wrong way round.
