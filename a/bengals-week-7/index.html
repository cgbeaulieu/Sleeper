Bengals Week 7:
Thirty-Five Unanswered

The Bengals were down 13-0 at halftime in Baltimore and won 35-13. Joe Burrow went 16 for 18 in the second half, Tee Higgins caught 148 yards, and TheSoop's quarterback and defence scored 43.38 between them.

Bengals 35, Ravens 13

Two halves

Baltimore had the first half. The Ravens led 13-0 at the break with Lamar Jackson out, and Zay Flowers had 120 receiving yards before halftime. Then the Bengals scored touchdowns on their first five possessions of the second half, the defence did not allow a point after the break, and Cincinnati won 35-13 for its first AFC North win of the season. The Bengals are 4-2 and Baltimore is 4-3.

Joe Burrow finished 22 of 30 for 292 yards and four touchdowns, to Tee Higgins, Mike Gesicki, Drew Sample and Ja'Marr Chase. In the second half he was 16 of 18 for 249 yards. It was his first game after the week 6 bye, and his second straight game with four touchdown passes to four different receivers. The house position on Burrow is unchanged and does not need these numbers, though it will take them.

The defence had four sacks, two of them by Boye Mafe, who has five on the season. Rookie Cashius Howell had his first career sack and Demetrius Knight Jr. intercepted a pass in the fourth quarter. Zac Taylor's summary afterwards was that the defence “got five straight stops.”

Higgins had 148 yards on six catches in OneManEra's flex, in the week OneManEra won his sixth straight result. All five Bengals on rosters were started, which is the correct number.

Three Bengals who scored are free agents here. Drew Sample scored 9.40 with his touchdown, Samaje Perine 8.60 and Mike Gesicki 7.10. Evan McPherson, dropped by geauxjack before week 7, scored 5.00.

The Ravens side went to a few rosters too. Flowers scored 25.70 for TheSoop, so he was paid by both teams in this game. Derrick Henry scored 6.40 for evspade, and ProfessorMerlyn's Ravens defence scored -1.00.
