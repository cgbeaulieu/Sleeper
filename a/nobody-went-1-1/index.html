Week 1

Five teams won twice and five lost twice

This league plays a median matchup, so every team gets two results a week. You play the opponent on your schedule, and you play the league's median score. The point of the second result is that scoring well and drawing the wrong opponent should not be the same thing as playing badly, and in a normal week several teams end up 1-1 because of it.

Week 1 was not a normal week. Every team that won its head-to-head also beat the median, and every team that lost its head-to-head also missed it. Five teams are 2-0 and five teams are 0-2. The median matchup was installed to strip some luck out of the standings and in its first week of the season it changed nothing at all.

The second result was supposed to be the merciful one. It agreed with the first one ten times out of ten.

The standings

That will not last. It holds this week only because nothing split, and the first team to score well in a loss will break it.

How close it came

Three teams missed a win by under three points

The perfect split looks decisive and it was not. The median landed at 152.15, and the three teams directly underneath it were 4th and 35 at 150.84, Fryder-Man at 149.80, and Rancor Fajitas at 149.70. All three were within 2.45 points of a median win, which would have made them 1-1 and broken the pattern.

Above the line it was just as tight. Bison Circle Buttercups cleared the median by 1.31 and beat Rancor Fajitas head-to-head by 3.76, which was the closest game of the week. CaptainClark won twice on a margin that a single reception would have erased.

The blowout ran the other way. Who Dey and the Blowfish put up 204.92, the highest score in the league, and beat Fryder-Man by 55.12. ceefry scored 149.80 in that game, which in most weeks is a respectable total and in this one was the seventh-best score and worth nothing.

The house note

The high score did not come from the quarterback

TheSoop's 204.92 was built on Josh Allen at 35.66, Ashton Jeanty at 33.20, David Montgomery at 28.40 and Zay Flowers at 25.50 before Flowers pulled up with a hamstring. Joe Burrow went for 15.16 in the superflex, which was a middling week by the standards of the slot and the fifth-lowest of the eleven players TheSoop started.

The team scored more points than anyone else in the league anyway. Burrow remains the greatest quarterback of all time and week 1 simply was not about him.
