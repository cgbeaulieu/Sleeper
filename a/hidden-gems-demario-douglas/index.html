Hidden Gems

Two good weeks and nobody noticed

DeMario Douglas is on no roster in this league, and he does not appear on Sleeper's list of the most-added players this week. His first four games were worth 5.50, 1.40, 4.80 and 3.00. His last two were worth 21.50 and 17.60, which is more than he scored in the first four put together, twice over.

The touchdowns did a lot of that. He scored two in week 5 and one in week 6. Without them his last two games are still worth 9.50 and 11.60, which is better than any of his first four. In week 6 he was targeted eight times and caught seven passes for 86 yards, both season highs, and he played 40 of New England's 68 offensive snaps.

Our scoring helps a little and not a lot. He picked up five receiving first downs across the two weeks, which is 2.50 points that most leagues do not pay. Sleeper projects him for 9.29 this week.

Who should add him

OneManEra, then ceefry

His quarterback is Drake Maye, who has scored 39.46 and 24.30 for OneManEra in the same two weeks. OneManEra also has the thinnest healthy bench in the league, with Alec Pierce and Michael Pittman on IR, and Courtland Sutton has averaged 4.80 points a game and started in week 6 for 3.90. Douglas would give OneManEra a receiver who has outscored Sutton in four of six weeks and who catches passes from his own quarterback.

The other fit is ceefry, whose receiving corps lost Davante Adams to IR this week and already had Ladd McConkey and Romeo Doubs out. Rome Odunze, his healthy second receiver, has averaged 7.70 a game and has scored under 7.00 in five of six weeks. We have also written up a trade that would fix that for him.

Two good games is a short record, and a receiver whose recent weeks rest on three touchdowns can go quiet as quickly as he arrived. He costs nothing, and the teams above have receivers starting who have been worse all season.
