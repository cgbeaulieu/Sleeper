What If: Lamar
For Chris Olave

We tested more than 10,000 one-for-one trades between rosters in this league. Exactly one makes both teams better on both of our measures, and it sends ceefry's quarterback to the team he beat by 6.92 last week.

What If

One trade out of ten thousand

This league has made four trades in four seasons, so this is a thought experiment. We paired up the league's rosters, swapped each skill player on one for each skill player on the other, rebuilt both teams' best legal lineups, and kept the trades that made both teams better. We used two measures, the season projections behind the power rankings and each player's actual points per game this season, and required both teams to improve on both.

More than 10,000 trades went in. One came out. ceefry sends Lamar Jackson to brunosilvadev for Chris Olave.

Fryder-Man: Brand New Day · 9-3

Why ceefry would do it

ceefry has five quarterbacks: Jackson, Baker Mayfield, Tyler Shough, Michael Penix and J.J. McCarthy. Only two can start. Jackson has the highest projection of the five, but he has not played since hurting his ankle on October 4, and the Ravens' coach called it "a day-by-day thing" after a limited practice on Wednesday. Without him, Shough and Penix scored 51.06 between them last week.

The receivers are where ceefry is short. Davante Adams is on IR for six to ten weeks with a ruptured hamstring, Ladd McConkey is out and Romeo Doubs is on IR. Olave has played all six games and averages 17.75 points a game, which is more than any healthy receiver ceefry has. On the season projections ceefry's lineup goes from 2539.5 to 2560.8, with Mayfield and Shough starting at quarterback and Olave at receiver. On points per game it goes from 181.0 to 181.6.

That second number is small, and it is the honest part of this. ceefry gains on both measures, but only because his quarterback depth means he loses very little when Jackson leaves. Olave also plays with Shough in New Orleans, so ceefry would be starting a quarterback and one of his own receivers.

Skinned Knee Varsity · 6-6

Why brunosilvadev would do it

brunosilvadev has the opposite problem. His quarterbacks are Matthew Stafford, Daniel Jones and Tanner McKee, the waiver pickup who took over in Philadelphia when Jalen Hurts went on IR. On points per game his superflex is McKee at 12.6. Jackson averages 21.02 in his four games.

He can afford to lose Olave because he has George Pickens, Jameson Williams and Stefon Diggs behind him. On the season projections his lineup goes from 2440.2 to 2476.6, the bigger gain of the two. On points per game it goes from 174.9 to 176.5.

The risk is all on his side. If Jackson's ankle keeps him out for longer, brunosilvadev has given up a receiver who has played every week for a quarterback who has not played since the first weekend of October. He would also be strengthening the team in first place, which beat him by 6.92 last week.

Will it happen

Probably not

The trade deadline is week 11. Four trades in four seasons says nobody will pick up the phone, and that is fine. The point of the search is that the league's best trade is one between the first-place team and a team at .500, built on five quarterbacks on one roster and three on the other.

Method: for pairs of rosters we swapped each quarterback, running back, receiver or tight end on one for each on the other, rebuilt both teams' best legal lineups, and kept only trades that raised both totals on two measures: Sleeper's full-season 2026 projections with players listed Out, IR, Doubtful or NA removed, and each player's actual 2026 points per game through week 6, both scored through config/scoring.json. This is the same lineup builder as the power rankings (scripts/roster_strength.py). Lamar Jackson is listed questionable and is counted. The points-per-game measure does not remove injured players, which is why Davante Adams and Jordan Mason still appear in ceefry's per-game lineup.
