Bengals Week 8:
Forty-Three In Overtime

The Bengals were down 13-0 after a quarter, gave the ball away four times, and beat the Titans 43-36 in overtime. Joe Burrow threw for 462 yards and five touchdowns, ran in the winner, and scored 42.48 for TheSoop.

Bengals 43, Titans 36, overtime

The long way round

Tennessee led 13-0 after the first quarter at Paycor Stadium and led 36-29 late in the fourth. The Bengals turned the ball over four times, three of them Joe Burrow interceptions, and Cam Ward threw for a career-high 304 yards against them. Burrow tied it with an 18-yard touchdown to Tee Higgins with 1:17 left and won it in overtime by running in from nine yards himself. The Bengals are 5-2.

Burrow finished 38 of 48 for 462 yards and five touchdowns, to Ja'Marr Chase from 30 yards, Jack Endries from one, Higgins twice and Mike Gesicki from 25. Higgins caught 10 passes for 155 yards and Chase 11 for 141. The defence forced two fumbles, a DJ Turner strip of Wan'Dale Robinson recovered by Josh Newton, and a Boye Mafe strip of Ward recovered by Dexter Lawrence.

Through our scoring, Burrow's day was 42.48 points, the best score by any player on any roster in this league in week 8. The three interceptions cost him three of them. We would like the record to show that he threw 48 passes and the other team caught three, and that he then won the game with his legs.

All five rostered Bengals started, and between them they scored 132.18. Higgins was the best starter on OneManEra's team and Chase the best on geauxjack's, but only one of them won. geauxjack scored the week's high of 174.30 and beat ceefry, and OneManEra lost to ProfessorMerlyn by 14.66, the closest game of the week, with 36.00 from Higgins and 29.18 from Kirk Cousins.

The other side of the game paid out too. Tony Pollard ran for 117 yards and two touchdowns and scored 28.20 for geauxjack, so the league's top score of the week got 56.80 from one game in Cincinnati. Wan'Dale Robinson caught a touchdown and lost the fumble, and scored 13.70 on TheSoop's bench.

Mike Gesicki, who caught the 25-yard touchdown, is still a free agent in this league, and Sleeper projects him for 10.53 this week, which is more than several started tight ends scored on Sunday.
