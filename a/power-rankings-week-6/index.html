Marking our own homework

The worst week these rankings have had

ceefry, our first, went 2-0. Teams two through six went a combined 1-9, and four of them lost both results. Teams seven through ten went 7-1. The team we ranked last, Willis’s Willies, scored the most points of the week and beat geauxjack for the first time in seven meetings.

We put KnightRider_85 tenth because Saquon Barkley, Breece Hall and DeVonta Smith were all hurt, and we wrote that the roster that had scored his points was no longer available to him. He scored 193.38 without any of them. Ollie Gordon, whose whole-season projection we quoted at 28.8 points, scored 17.30 at running back. The injury list has not changed, and the conclusion we drew from it was wrong.

What moved

Where we disagree with the table

The table has KnightRider_85, TheSoop and geauxjack tied at 6-4 behind ceefry, and so do we, roughly. The disagreement is further down. CaptainClark is 4-6 and ninth in points and we have him sixth, because by both of our strength measures his roster is fourth in the league. derrek777 is 4-6 and we have him fourth, because his healthy roster projects second and his bench is the deepest in the league. brunosilvadev is 5-5, fifth in the table and seventh here, because his bench has nothing in it.

We also moved KnightRider_85 up five places in a week when his projected lineup is the worst in the league. That is because the projection is about one bye week, and the ranking is about the nine weeks of regular season left.
