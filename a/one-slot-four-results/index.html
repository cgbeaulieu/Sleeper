Four Results

Rancor Fajitas

The tight end scored nothing

ProfessorMerlyn lost to Bison Circle Buttercups by 3.76 points, 149.70 to 153.46. It was the closest game of the week. He started Colston Loveland at tight end and Loveland finished with 0.00, and Travis Kelce sat on his bench with 9.60.

Swap those two and Merlyn scores 159.30, which beats CaptainClark comfortably. That is the obvious part. The part that makes it expensive is the median.

The arithmetic

Why it moved four results and not one

The league median is the middle of all ten scores, so changing one team's total also moves the line everyone else is measured against. Merlyn scored 149.70 and the median came in at 152.15. He missed his second result by 2.45 points and went 0-2.

Put Kelce in the lineup and he scores 159.30. The median recomputes to 155.99, and Merlyn clears it. He goes from 0-2 to 2-0. CaptainClark, who scored 153.46 and cleared the old median by 1.31, now sits below the new one and drops from 2-0 to 1-1.

Four results on one roster slot, in week 1, from a player who was already on the roster. The median matchup is usually described as the rule that protects you from bad luck, and it is also the rule that doubles the cost of a lineup mistake.

The other nine slots

He got the hard call right

The rest of the lineup was good, and one call in it was the best in the league. Merlyn started Bryce Young in the superflex and Young put up 32.44, which was the highest score any team got from that slot in week 1. He did that with Patrick Mahomes available on his bench, where Mahomes scored 23.16. Starting Young over Mahomes was the kind of decision that loses you the week when it goes wrong and Merlyn was right by more than nine points.

Christian Watson added 31.20 and DJ Moore 20.00. Rancor Fajitas was a top-half offense in every part of the roster that does not involve a tight end.

Mike Gesicki scored 18.30 at tight end in week 1 and is a free agent in this league.

Stafford watch

The quarterback he lost had a worse day than he did

Merlyn spent the draft losing Matthew Stafford to brunosilvadev at pick 48 and said in the league chat that it might be the first year he did not have his boy Stafford. He took Mahomes four picks later and then benched him.

Stafford scored 5.10 for Skinned Knee Varsity, who finished last in the league at 137.14 and also went 0-2. Both men are winless, both are carrying a quarterback decision that did not work, and they play each other in week 2.
