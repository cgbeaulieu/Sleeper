Week 3

Five sweeps and five shutouts

The league median in week 3 was 149.38. Every team that won its head-to-head game scored above it and every team that lost scored below it, so the week produced five 2-0 records and five 0-2 records and nothing in between. That also happened in week 1. Of the 30 team-weeks played so far, four have ended 1-1, and all four came in week 2.

The format is meant to separate a team that lost to the week's best score from a team that was bad. This week it had nothing to separate. The five losers were the five lowest scorers.

The top

ceefry finally scored like his roster

Fryder-Man scored 217.99, the highest total of 2026 by 13.07 over TheSoop's week 1 score, and beat Rancor Fajitas by 77.61, the biggest margin of the week. Bijan Robinson had 39.30, Tyler Shough 25.30 in his first superflex start of the season, Garrett Wilson 24.20, and Kyren Williams, Jaylen Warren and Juwan Johnson all cleared 20. The worst starter on the roster was the kicker, Trey Smack, at 1.75.

It still falls 28 points short of ceefry's own league record, the 246.3 he scored as a six seed in the 2024 playoffs on the way to the title. It does make him the league's leader in points scored at 507.5, and he is 3-3.

CaptainClark had the second-best week at 181.97 and beat OneManEra by 66.79. Two weeks ago he left 99.00 on his bench. This time he left 54.98, including 24.18 from Aaron Rodgers, and it did not matter.

The bottom

The best player of the week lost twice

Jahmyr Gibbs scored 41.90, more than any other starter in the league, and 4th and 35 lost to Who Dey and the Blowfish by 26.14 and missed the median by 13.78. The rest of the lineup put up 93.70. The waiver piece this week covers where the other points were.

OneManEra's 115.18 is the lowest score of the season, 16.02 below the previous low. His two running backs combined for 9.70, Drake Maye scored 6.76, and Kirk Cousins scored 22.22 on the bench for the second week in a row. Drake London had 26.40.

Willis’s Willies lost their first results of the season, to Skinned Knee Varsity by 15.72. Sam Darnold returned and scored 28.16, and the rest of the roster did not come with him. brunosilvadev's $13 Minnesota defence scored 23.00. Matthew Stafford had 21.90, which will not have escaped the attention of ProfessorMerlyn, who lost by 77.61 the same week.

The closest game was geauxjack over the defending champion by 13.96. geauxjack is now 5-1 and alone at the top of the table. Four teams sit one result behind him at 4-2.
