OneManEra

The pickup worked

Two days after the draft, OneManEra added Kirk Cousins as a free agent. Sleeper's preseason projection had Cousins at 39.5 points for the entire season, which is what a backup projects for. After four weeks he has 82.84, an average of 20.71.

That is more than either of the quarterbacks OneManEra has started every week. Jalen Hurts has 73.52 and Drake Maye has 60.76. Cousins has scored all 82.84 on the bench.

Week 1 was by 0.48 and nobody could have called it. Weeks 2 and 3 were not close. In week 4 Maye finally had his game, 29.16, and that was the week Cousins outscored Hurts instead.

What it was worth

One result, and it came in week 2

Starting the best two of the three each week would have added 37.22 points across the season. Most of that did not change anything. OneManEra lost to KnightRider_85 by 15.87 in week 1, to CaptainClark by 66.79 in week 3 and to TheSoop by 14.86 in week 4, and the Cousins swap does not close any of those.

Week 2 is the exception. OneManEra lost to derrek777 145.44 to 141.98. Cousins in place of Maye makes it 153.68, a win by 8.24. He had already beaten the median that week, so it turns 1-1 into 2-0, and derrek777 goes from 2-0 to 1-1. The recomputed median moves from 141.40 to 142.34 and nobody else's result changes.

This week

The projections still say bench him

Sleeper's week 5 projections, on our scoring, have Maye at 21.2, Hurts at 17.6 and Cousins at 15.0. Those are the numbers OneManEra has been following, and for four weeks running they have undersold the same player.

None of this is a bad roster. Its healthy lineup projects fifth in the league, Maye's 29.16 was the best score on it last week, and the team that lost to the week's high score in week 4 still beat the median. OneManEra plays brunosilvadev this week at 2-6, and he has three quarterbacks worth starting for two places. Most of this league would take that problem. He is also still the only founder with a ring.
