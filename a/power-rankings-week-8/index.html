Marking our own homework

Seven and three

Our top five went 7-3 in week 7 and our bottom five went 3-7. TheSoop and geauxjack, our third and fourth, both went 2-0, and ProfessorMerlyn and CaptainClark, our ninth and tenth, both went 0-2.

The disagreements with the table were mixed. We had evspade fifth and derrek777 sixth on roster strength, and each won his game and missed the median, so 1-1 apiece. We had OneManEra eighth because his roster scores the fewest points per week in the league, and he went 2-0 for the third straight week. He moves up three places this time and we explain below why it is not more.

What moved

Where we disagree with the table

The table has ceefry first by a result, and we have him second behind TheSoop because TheSoop's lineup scores 10.5 more points a week and ceefry has Lamar Jackson and three receivers out. The three teams at 9-5 are in a different order from the table's tiebreak, with KnightRider_85 last of the three because he left the most points on his bench this week and started a running back who did not play for the second week running. derrek777 is 5-9 and we have him above evspade at 6-8, which is a vote for the healthier roster with the better week 8 projection.

Seven weeks remain before the playoffs and six of ten teams get in. OneManEra in fifth is two results clear of brunosilvadev and evspade in sixth, and derrek777 is one result behind them.
