The format

Everybody plays twice

The median matchup is on, so these five games are only half of what happens. Every team also plays the league median score, and a week produces two results instead of one. A 6-4 head-to-head record and a 6-4 median record are separate ledgers that add together.

Beating your opponent is worth exactly as much as outscoring five of the other nine teams. A lucky week against a cold opponent counts once instead of twice.

That rule arrived in 2025 and it changed what a record looks like. evspade went 24-4 that season, which is 14 weeks of results rather than 28 games. Those numbers do not line up against 2024 and earlier, and comparing them directly gets the history wrong.

What to watch

The superflex experiment starts now

Two teams are opening with a wide receiver in the superflex slot. Willis's Willies is starting DK Metcalf there and Fryder-Man is starting Rome Odunze. In a format that pays half a point for rushing first downs and nothing for passing first downs, both of those may be correct.

The cleanest read on the season comes from tracking those two against the teams that started two real quarterbacks. LeBron Offensive Foul is the only roster starting two mobile ones, and it is the roster that won last year.
