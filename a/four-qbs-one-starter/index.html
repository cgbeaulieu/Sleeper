Cleveland, Ohio

The good part came first

KnightRider_85 had a great draft for six rounds. Jaxon Smith-Njigba at 6, Saquon Barkley at 15, then Chase Brown, Malik Nabers, Breece Hall and DeVonta Smith. No reaches, no panic, and one of the best groups of skill players in the league.

Then he waited until pick 66 to take a quarterback, which is a long time to wait in a superflex league. The pick was Sam Darnold. He followed it with Jared Goff, Malik Willis and C.J. Stroud.

66 · Sam Darnold 75 · Jared Goff 106 · Malik Willis 115 · C.J. Stroud

Four quarterbacks, none of them good. The Browns have been running this experiment since 1999 and it has not worked there either. For a guy with Knight Rider in his username, he has assembled a garage full of cars that will not start.

He also named the team Willis's Willies and then drafted Malik Willis in round 11. Willis is currently Miami's backup, so the name is more of a goal than a description.

The Week 1 lineup is the part worth sitting with. After spending four picks on the position he is starting DK Metcalf in the superflex, with Darnold, Willis and Stroud on the bench. He is probably right to do it, because Metcalf is the better play. Four quarterback picks bought him one starter.

The defense

He has done this before and it worked

In 2025 he waited until pick 82 for his first quarterback, longer than anyone else in the league, and finished third. That was his best result in five seasons, and it came the year after he went 2-12 and finished dead last.

The two managers who waited longest on quarterback in 2025 finished first and third. KnightRider_85 was the one who waited longest of all.

So the roast has a real answer to it. He is repeating a plan that produced the best season of his career, in a format where the podium went to the teams that ignored the position early. The difference this year is that four picks went into the room instead of one or two, and three of those picks are inactive on his own bench in Week 1.
