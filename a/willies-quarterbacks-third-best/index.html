Four quarterbacks and none of them good

KnightRider_85 drafted four quarterbacks in September: Sam Darnold at 66, Jared Goff at 75, Malik Willis at 106 and C.J. Stroud at 115. The commissioner called him the Browns of The Road To Glory on draft night, and in week 1 Darnold scored 0.52 at superflex and the two quarterback slots produced 16.96 points, ninth of ten teams. This site has come back to the line many times since.

We have pointed out his bad quarterback weeks as they happened, but we had not added up the whole season. We have now, and the line does not hold up.

Week 1 was the worst week his quarterbacks have had. In the nine weeks since, they have been above the league median seven times, and in week 3, when Goff scored 19.36 and Darnold 28.16, they were the best pair in the league. Over the season the two slots have scored 397.84 points, which is 22.08 more than a team scoring the median every week would have.

Only two teams have more. TheSoop has 518.28 from Joe Burrow and Josh Allen, far ahead of everybody, and geauxjack has 416.42. KnightRider_85 is third, ahead of OneManEra, CaptainClark and the other five. The defending champion, evspade, is tenth with 286.74.

How it happened

Stroud, mostly

The quarterback he took last, at 115, has started in the QB or superflex slot in seven of the last nine weeks. Stroud scored 37.36 in week 9, the best score by any player in the league that week, and 21.94 on Sunday. Goff has started in nine of the ten weeks, and his low weeks have been very low: 10.54 in week 8 and 6.80 in week 9. On Sunday he scored 22.92, his best since week 5.

Willis, the fourth quarterback, went to brunosilvadev in the trade at the end of October, and Darnold has missed two games with quadriceps and rib injuries. Seattle is on bye this week. After Sunday's game Mike Macdonald said he did not yet know whether Darnold would be back in week 12, and that how much he can practise after the bye will decide it.

The other half of the original joke was that he would start a wide receiver at superflex. He never has. In week 1 every team in the league started a quarterback there, and KnightRider_85 has done it every week since.

Week 10

80.51

On Sunday he beat OneManEra 195.81 to 115.30. The margin is the widest of the season, ahead of ceefry's 77.61 over ProfessorMerlyn in week 3. Stroud and Goff scored 44.86, Jaxon Smith-Njigba 29.60, Chase Brown 26.80 with two touchdown catches against Pittsburgh, and Isaiah Likely 24.20. Breece Hall, who started in weeks 6, 7 and 8 and scored 0.00 in each of them, started again and scored 15.80. KnightRider_85 is 11-9, fourth in the table, and leads OneManEra 7-3 all time.

We are not retiring the Browns line, because the commissioner likes it and because KnightRider_85 picked up Jason Myers at kicker last week, a few days before Seattle's bye. But the quarterbacks have been the third-best pair in the league this season, and we should have said so before now.
