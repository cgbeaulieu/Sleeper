Eight starters

Willis’s Willies played week 6 with eight starters. Saquon Barkley, who has not played since hurting his hamstring in week 4, started at one running back spot. Breece Hall, who has not played since a quad injury in week 3, started at the other. Neither has an entry in Sleeper's stats for the week, and both scored 0.00. The kicker slot was empty.

Last week we noted that Sleeper's projection for his lineup had nobody at either running back slot, because Miami and Cincinnati were on bye and took Ollie Gordon and Chase Brown with them. KnightRider_85 had no healthy running back on the roster to start, which is a fair excuse for the running back spots. Dameon Pierce was a free agent until Sunday morning, when OneManEra picked him up and started him for 15.00.

The kicker is harder to explain. After week 5 he dropped Riley Patterson, whose Dolphins were on bye, and claimed Cairo Santos on waivers for $5. Santos kicked for Chicago on Sunday and scored 9.00 on the Willies bench. The Broncos defence, also on the bench, scored 20.00 while the Seahawks defence started and scored 5.00.

How it won anyway

The receivers

DeVonta Smith, back from his own injury, added 18.00 and DK Metcalf 17.30 in the flex spots. Sam Darnold scored 13.52 at superflex.

derrek777 scored 130.94 without Jahmyr Gibbs, who was on bye. Jordan Love scored 21.74, Jayden Daniels 21.00 and Carnell Tate 17.50, but MarShawn Lloyd scored 1.00 at running back and Omarion Hampton 6.40. Week 6 was the lowest-scoring week of the season, with a median of 135.34, and in that week a team with three empty slots won by the largest margin of any game.

With Santos and the Broncos in the lineup, the Willies score 174.10. That would have been the highest score of the week by 20.54 and a 43.16-point win. As it is, KnightRider_85 is 1-0 against derrek777, has won four straight results, and sits second at 8-4.

This week

The running backs might be back

Barkley practised on Thursday for the first time since week 4, and NBC Sports reports he is on track to play on Monday night against Dallas. Hall did not practise on Wednesday. Gordon and Chase Brown are back from their byes, so for the first time since September he has four running backs who could play. He plays TheSoop, who is 5-1 against him all time.
