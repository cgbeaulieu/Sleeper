Eight starters

Willis’s Willies played week 6 with eight starters. Saquon Barkley, who has not played since hurting his hamstring in week 4, started at one running back spot. Breece Hall, who has not played since a quad injury in week 3, started at the other. Neither has an entry in Sleeper's stats for the week, and both scored 0.00. The kicker slot was empty.

Last week we noted that Sleeper's projection for his lineup had nobody at either running back slot, because Miami and Cincinnati were on bye and took Ollie Gordon and Chase Brown with them. KnightRider_85 had no healthy running back on the roster to start, which is a fair excuse for the running back spots. Dameon Pierce was a free agent until Sunday morning, when OneManEra picked him up and started him for 15.00.

The kicker is harder to explain. After week 5 he dropped Riley Patterson, whose Dolphins were on bye, and claimed Cairo Santos on waivers for $5.
