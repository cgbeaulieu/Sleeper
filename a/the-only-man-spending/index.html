Skinned Knee Varsity

The Stafford game went to the man who took him

ProfessorMerlyn lost Matthew Stafford to brunosilvadev at pick 48 of the draft and said in the league chat that it might be the first year he did not have his boy Stafford. The schedule put them together in week 2. Stafford threw for 27.98, Skinned Knee Varsity won 169.45 to 143.86, and it was the highest score anybody posted all week.

Merlyn did not play badly. He scored the fourth-most points in the league and beat the median. He just ran into the one team that scored more than anyone, on the one week where the quarterback in question turned up.

The league's waiver wire

Nine teams have not spent a dollar

Every manager in this league has a hundred dollars of FAAB. Two weeks in, the league has spent seventeen of a possible thousand, and all seventeen belong to brunosilvadev. He has also made the only three waiver claims anyone has submitted. The other eleven roster moves in the league were free-agent adds that cost nothing and beat nobody to the player.

He bid $11 on Carson Wentz while Kyler Murray was in concussion protocol, $6 on the Tampa Bay defence, and nothing on Mike Gesicki, who cost him only the roster spot occupied by Kyle Pitts and his 0.00. He then dropped Gesicki again for Dalton Schultz, who scored 23.00 at tight end in a lineup that had got nothing at all from the position in week 1.

Only one of them has paid so far. Schultz returned 23.00. Wentz started in the superflex and returned 6.82, which is the risk in buying a backup quarterback on a team whose coach has just called the offence a problem, and the Tampa defence he paid $6 for gave him 4.00. He spent seventeen dollars and the best thing he got was the free one.

The history

Churn has never won anything here

There is a case for the other nine. In 2025 CaptainClark reached the championship game on three adds and no FAAB at all, TheSoop led the league with 43 adds and missed the playoffs, and brunosilvadev himself spent the entire hundred dollars and finished fifth. The champion sat mid-pack. Activity has never correlated with anything good in this league.

What is different this year is that he is the one manager with a structural hole. He owns two quarterbacks in a superflex league and they project for 514.3 points between them, 77 behind the next-worst room. Nobody is trading with him, because this league has completed four trades in its entire history and all four were in 2023. Waivers are the only door.

He is 2-2 with the fourth-most points in the league and the roster we ranked eighth. Whatever the history says about churn, the man with the actual problem is the only one doing anything about it.
