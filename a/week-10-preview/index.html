Where it stands

Five weeks and ten results remain before the playoffs, and six of ten teams get in. ceefry, geauxjack and TheSoop are tied at 12-6, in that order on points. KnightRider_85 and evspade are 9-9. derrek777, OneManEra and ProfessorMerlyn are 8-10 and derrek777 holds sixth on points, 32.6 ahead of OneManEra. brunosilvadev and CaptainClark are 6-12. The trade deadline is week 11.

Chicago, Denver, Philadelphia and Tampa Bay are on bye. ProfessorMerlyn loses Bucky Irving and Jonah Coleman, who scored 46.80 between them in his week-high 173.22. evspade loses Bo Nix, and with Kyler Murray listed out that leaves Drew Lock as his only quarterback with a game. KnightRider_85 loses Saquon Barkley, his kicker Cairo Santos and the Broncos defence. CaptainClark loses Caleb Williams and Chris Godwin, and geauxjack loses D'Andre Swift and the Bears defence that scored 20.00 for him last week. brunosilvadev is the only team with nobody on bye.
