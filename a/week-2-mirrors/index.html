The marquee

Rancor Fajitas and the quarterback that got away

ProfessorMerlyn has had Matthew Stafford for most of the life of this league and lost him in the 2026 draft when brunosilvadev took him at pick 48. Merlyn said in the league chat at the time that it might be the first year he did not have his boy Stafford, and then took Patrick Mahomes four picks later. They play each other this week.

Both are 0-2 and both have a reason to feel hard done by. Merlyn scored the eighth-most points in the league and lost by 3.76 with Travis Kelce on his bench. brunosilvadev scored the fewest points of anyone, 137.14, and got 15.64 out of his two quarterback slots, which was the worst in the league. Stafford supplied 5.10 of that.

Merlyn has never beaten him. The all-time record is 0-2 in brunosilvadev's favour, which is a small sample from a manager who only joined in 2024, and it is still the record.

The 2-0 games

The two highest scores in the league meet

Who Dey and the Blowfish put up 204.92 in week 1 and Irrational Exuberance! put up 182.10, the top two totals by a distance, and the schedule has them playing each other immediately. One of them will be 2-2 by Monday and it will not mean much about either roster.

TheSoop is 2-4 against geauxjack all time. Both are founders, both have played all five seasons, and neither has won anything, which is true of five of the six managers who have been here since 2022.

The other 2-0 game has more history behind it. KnightRider_85 is 6-1 against CaptainClark across four seasons, part of a chain where geauxjack beats KnightRider_85, KnightRider_85 beats CaptainClark, and geauxjack beats CaptainClark as well. CaptainClark got 62.36 from his two quarterback slots in week 1, the most in the league, on Caleb Williams at 38.26 and Brock Purdy at 24.10.

The champions' game

2024 against 2025

ceefry won the title in 2024 from the sixth seed with a 6-8 record, and evspade won it in 2025 as the first seed with the most points in the league. They are the only two managers in this game with rings, and they meet in week 2 with ceefry 0-2 and evspade 2-0.

ceefry is 1-4 against evspade all time. He also scored 149.80 in week 1, which was the seventh-best total in the league and still lost by 55.12, and he did it with Tyler Shough and his 25.70 sitting on the bench behind Baker Mayfield at 11.64.

OneManEra and 4th and 35 have never played each other. derrek777 joined this season, so this is the first meeting between them, and one of the two will have a win by the end of it.

Quarterback questions

Three starters are in doubt and one replacement is free

Sam Darnold left week 1 with a glute injury after Seattle feared a fractured hip, and his coach said afterwards that he did not know the extent of it. Darnold scored 0.52 in the superflex for Willis’s Willies. KnightRider_85 has Malik Willis and C.J. Stroud on his bench and is the one manager in the league already set up for this.

Kyler Murray took a hit to the head on his eleventh snap as a Viking and is in concussion protocol, day to day. He scored 0.62 for the defending champion. If Murray cannot go, Carson Wentz starts for Minnesota, and Wentz scored 19.72 in relief in week 1 and is a free agent in this league. evspade has Bo Nix behind Murray and a decision to make.

Zay Flowers is day to day with a hamstring after catching five passes for 150 yards and a touchdown inside 25 minutes for TheSoop. Mike Gesicki scored 18.30 at tight end and is also unrostered here, which the two teams that got 0.00 out of the position in week 1 might want to look at.
