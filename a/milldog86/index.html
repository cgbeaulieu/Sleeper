David Miller

Milldog86. Proud manager of the Miller HighLife Ballers.

In this piece

On December 15, 2023, our league lost a dear friend when David Miller, proud manager of the Miller HighLife Ballers, passed away.

For many of us the loss was deeply personal, as our friendships with David traced all the way back to our high school days. He brought incredible joy to our lives, leaving us with countless memories of fierce 1-on-1 basketball games, unforgettable moments on the golf course, and a signature, cheesy grin we will never forget. We miss his humor, his classic sayings, and his friendship deeply, but we take comfort in knowing he has moved on to a better place.

David left us right in the middle of the 2023 fantasy playoffs, just after his 37th birthday. As the 5 seed, his team was still in the hunt. In his honor the entire league rallied together to keep the Miller HighLife Ballers alive. With the commissioner managing the roster and leaguemates pitching in to flag injuries and make necessary lineup adjustments, David’s team went on an incredible run and won the championship.

It was the ultimate fantasy victory, and we know he would have been thrilled to see his team take home the trophy, especially against his longtime rival, OneManEra.

Following the season, the league sent David’s championship winnings to his fiancée, hoping to provide a small bit of support during a difficult time.

Cheers to a great season, Miller. We miss you, man.
