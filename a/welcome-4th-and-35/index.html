Welcome to The Road To Glory

There is one new manager in the league this season. derrek777 takes over as 4th and 35, joining nine returners who have been at this since 2022, and the draft handed him the first overall pick on his way in.

He used it on Jahmyr Gibbs. Then he came back at 20 and 21 with Omarion Hampton and Jayden Daniels, which is a running back and a quarterback who both do the thing this league pays for.

He named the team for the worst down and distance in football and then drew the best pick in the draft.

The roster

The Daniels pick fits the format

Our scoring gives half a point for rushing first downs and nothing for passing first downs. That makes quarterbacks who run worth more here than they are anywhere else, and Jayden Daniels at pick 21 is the fourth-earliest quarterback taken in the league.

The superflex slot is the other half of it. Nine of ten teams needed a second quarterback and twelve of them ended up on benches around the league. His came at pick 80, where the autodraft stepped in and took Jordan Love. The computer picked a starting NFL quarterback with that slot, which is more than several of the humans managed.

The debut

He opens as the biggest favorite in the league

Week 1 puts 4th and 35 against Irrational Exuberance! at 64%. That is the largest edge on the board, ahead of the defending champion at 56%. No other game is outside a four-point swing of even.

The history is not on his side, and it is not badly against him either. Four managers have joined this league since the opening season and none has won a title in their first year. ceefry needed two seasons and evspade needed three, and both of them have rings now.

The league has also produced four different champions in four seasons and has never had a repeat, so the door is not exactly guarded. A first-year manager with the first overall pick and the week's biggest edge is a reasonable place to start.
