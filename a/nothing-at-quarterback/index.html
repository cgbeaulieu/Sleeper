Willis’s Willies

The superflex scored 0.52

KnightRider_85 drafted four quarterbacks and started the one who got hurt. Sam Darnold left Seattle's game in the first half with a glute injury and finished with 0.52 points, which was the lowest score any team got from the superflex slot in week 1. Jared Goff added 16.44 at quarterback proper.

That is 16.96 from the two slots this format is built around, ninth out of ten teams. Only brunosilvadev, who owns two quarterbacks in total and started both of them, did worse.

On the bench were Malik Willis with 18.20 and C.J. Stroud with 17.00. Either one of them, on his own, outscored both of the quarterbacks who played.

The result

He won by 15.87 and beat the median

Willis’s Willies beat OneManEra 158.51 to 142.64 and cleared the league median by 6.36, so the worst quarterback week in the league outside of last place turned into a 2-0 start. The team sits fourth.

The rest of the roster did the work. Isaiah Likely went for 25.80 at tight end, Jaxon Smith-Njigba 24.20, Breece Hall 21.80 off the flex, and Chase Brown 17.80. Four players outscored the entire quarterback room combined.

He waited until pick 66 to take a quarterback in 2026. He waited until pick 82 in 2025 and finished third.

This is the second season in a row he has built a roster that looks wrong on draft night and works on Sunday. The pattern held for a full season last year and it is one week old this year, so it proves nothing yet, and it is the same pattern.

The hoard

The four quarterbacks are about to be useful

The joke about this roster since September has been that it contains four quarterbacks and no good ones. Goff at 75, Darnold at 66, Malik Willis at 106 and Stroud at 115, none of them a player anyone else in the league wanted early.

Darnold's status for week 2 is unclear. Seattle ruled out a fractured hip and his coach said he did not know how long it would be. Every other team in this league carrying an injured starting quarterback has a problem this week. KnightRider_85 has two replacements who already outscored the man they would replace, and he does not have to spend a dollar of FAAB or make a trade, which is fortunate given that this league has completed four trades in its entire history.

Malik Willis remains the only Willis in Willis’s Willies, and in week 1 he was also the best quarterback on the team. He watched the whole thing from the bench.

Week 2

Bison Circle Buttercups, and the usual outcome

He draws CaptainClark, who is also 2-0 and who got 62.36 out of his quarterback slots in week 1, the most in the league. That is a 45-point swing at the position in CaptainClark's favour if both weeks repeat.

KnightRider_85 is 6-1 against him all time. CaptainClark has lost six of the seven meetings, and last week KnightRider_85 beat the median with a quarterback who was on the sideline by half time.
